The House

A private house, not a salon.

LUMÉ was founded on a simple conviction: that beauty care deserves the same attention as a well-kept home. Every ritual is unhurried, every specialist trained to listen before they begin.

Our rooms are quiet by design. Natural light, considered materials, and a philosophy that restraint is its own form of luxury — this is the standard we hold ourselves to, appointment after appointment.
