Privacy Policy

What information we collect

When you book an appointment with us, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and — if you choose to provide them — your date of birth and any notes or special requests (for example, allergies or preferences relevant to your service).

Why we collect it

  • To create, confirm, reschedule, and manage your appointments
  • To send you confirmations and reminders by email, and by WhatsApp if you choose to use that option
  • To keep a record of your visit history so we can serve you better on future visits

Who we share it with

We use trusted third-party services to run this booking system — a database and hosting provider to securely store your information, and an email delivery service to send confirmations and reminders. We do not sell your information to anyone, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

How we protect it

Your information is stored with access controls that limit who on our team can see it, based on their role. Connections to our booking system are encrypted. No system is perfectly unbreakable, but we take reasonable, real technical steps to protect your data — not just a promise on paper.

How long we keep it

We keep your booking information for as long as you're an active customer, so we can maintain your visit history. You can ask us to delete your information at any time — see "Your rights" below.

Cookies and local storage

This site stores a small amount of information directly on your own device — such as your language preference and your details for faster rebooking — so you don't have to re-enter them. This stays on your device and isn't used to track you across other websites.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it entirely. Just reach out using the contact details below.

Terms of Service

Booking

By booking an appointment through this site, you confirm the details you provide are accurate. We reserve time and staff specifically for your appointment, so please let us know as early as possible if your plans change.

Cancellations and rescheduling

You can cancel or reschedule your appointment yourself through the confirmation link sent to you, or by looking up your booking with your phone number. Online changes may close a short window before your appointment time — after that, please contact us directly.

No-shows

If you're unable to make your appointment and don't cancel in advance, we may not be able to offer that time to another customer. Repeated no-shows may affect our ability to offer you future online bookings.

Liability

We provide our services with reasonable care and skill. To the extent permitted by law, we aren't liable for indirect or consequential loss arising from your use of this booking system.

Changes to these terms
